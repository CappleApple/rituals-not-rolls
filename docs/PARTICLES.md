# Enchantment particle themes

The 68 bundled presets use 40 native Minecraft particle types across 31 sprite sets, with enchantment-specific tints. Ritual particles render at full brightness so their shapes remain readable in dim libraries. Large blasts, slashes and gusts are reduced to fit ritual trails. The table lists the intended visual theme; item sources, powers and level thresholds are unchanged.

Book placement and retrieval retain their existing effects. No enchanting preset uses the `minecraft:enchant` rune particle.
